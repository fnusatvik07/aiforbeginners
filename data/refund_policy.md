# Nimbus Retail: Returns & Refunds Policy
Last updated: 3 March 2026

## Return window
Customers may return most items within **30 days of delivery**. The 30 days are counted
from the delivery date recorded by the courier, not the order date.

Perishable goods, personalised items, and opened software licences cannot be returned.

## Condition requirements
Items must be unopened, unused, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.
Items returned in a used condition may be refused, or refunded at 50% of the paid value.

## Refund processing time
Once a returned item is received and inspected at our Pune warehouse, refunds are issued
within **14 business days**. The money reaches the original payment method 3-5 business
days after we issue it, depending on the customer's bank.

## Refund method
Refunds are always issued to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different
card, a different UPI ID, or as store credit unless the customer explicitly requests store
credit, in which case the refund is instant and carries a 5% bonus.

## Shipping charges
Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error, a wrong
item, a damaged item, or a late delivery beyond the promised date. Customer-initiated
returns do not include a refund of the original shipping fee.

## Exchanges
We do not process exchanges. The customer should return the item for a refund and place a
new order. Orders above Rs 2,000 qualify for free return pickup.
