# Nimbus Retail: Shipping FAQ
Last updated: 18 February 2026

## How long does delivery take?
Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune):
2-3 business days. Tier-2 cities: 4-6 business days. Remote pin codes: up to 10 business
days. Business days exclude Sundays and national holidays.

## Do you offer express delivery?
Yes. Nimbus Express delivers next-day in metro cities for a flat Rs 149. Orders must be
placed before 2:00 PM IST to qualify for next-day delivery. Express is not available for
bulky items over 15 kg.

## Free shipping threshold
Orders above **Rs 999** ship free on standard delivery. Below that, standard shipping is
Rs 49 flat across India.

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is sent by SMS and email once the courier scans the parcel, usually within
12 hours of dispatch. Tracking information can take a further 6 hours to appear on the
courier's own site.

## My order is late
If an order has not moved for 4 consecutive days, customers should contact support.
Orders delayed beyond the promised window by more than 5 business days are eligible for
a full refund of shipping charges, and the customer may cancel for a full refund.

## International shipping
We currently ship only within India. We do not ship to international addresses, including
to freight forwarders.
