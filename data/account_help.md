# Nimbus Retail: Account & Payments Help
Last updated: 27 January 2026

## Accepted payment methods
UPI, credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking from 42 banks,
and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available on orders up to Rs 5,000 and carries
a Rs 29 handling fee.

## Why was my card declined?
The three most common causes are: an expired card, an incorrect CVV, and the bank's own
risk checks rejecting an online transaction. Fuel-pump and toll transactions often place
a temporary pre-authorisation hold that reduces the available limit for up to 72 hours,
which can cause an unrelated online payment to fail.

Customers should try a different payment method, or contact their bank. Nimbus never sees
the reason a bank declines a transaction.

## Failed payment but money debited
If money was debited and the order did not confirm, the transaction was auto-reversed by
the payment gateway. Banks typically credit the amount back within 5-7 business days with
no action needed from the customer or from Nimbus.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed from the Orders page until the parcel is dispatched.
After dispatch, the address cannot be changed. The customer must let the delivery fail and
reorder.

## Deleting an account
Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Order history is retained for
7 years for tax and audit purposes, as required by Indian law.
